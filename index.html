Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about booking private chauffeur and luxury limousine services in Dubai.

Are your drivers English speaking and RTA certified?

Yes. All our chauffeurs are licensed by the Dubai Road and Transport Authority (RTA), speak fluent English, and undergo extensive hospitality and safe driving background checks.

Are fuel and Salik toll charges included in the price?

Yes. All quoted daily (5-hour and 10-hour) and direct transfer rates within Dubai include fuel, Salik toll gate passes, and standard public parking.

How does airport flight tracking work if my flight is delayed?

When you provide your flight number, our dispatch team tracks the incoming flight telemetry live. If your flight is delayed or lands early, your chauffeur's arrival time is automatically updated at zero extra charge.

Can we make multiple stops during a 5-hour or 10-hour booking?

Absolutely. During your booked duration, the car and driver are dedicated solely to you. You can make unlimited stops for shopping, dining, business meetings, or sightseeing throughout Dubai.

Do you provide inter-emirate trips to Abu Dhabi or Oman?

Yes. We operate across all 7 Emirates including Abu Dhabi (Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, Louvre), Ras Al Khaimah, and Fujairah, as well as cross-border travel to Oman (Khasab / Musandam).

How quickly can a vehicle be dispatched?

For executive sedans and SUVs, we can often dispatch within 45–60 minutes in Dubai. For luxury VIP vans (Mercedes V-Class) or large coaches, we recommend reserving 12–24 hours in advance.
